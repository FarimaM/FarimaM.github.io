Research Interests & Vision

My work links model-based signal processing in Systems Neuroscience, Cognitive & Behavioral Neuroscience, and Clinical & Translational Neuroscience with machine learning—predictive modeling, neural decoding, and graph neural networks.

"I answer two basic concerns by identifying patterns in multimodal neuroimaging and electrophysiological datasets: How do intricate brain networks combine data to produce adaptive behavior? In order to understand and forecast biological brain dynamics, how can we develop coherent computational models?"

Research Projects & Experience

Latent Mean-Covariance Modeling for Strategic Foraging

Winter 2026 - Present

Research Collaborator | Interdisciplinary Schools - Tehran, Iran (Supervisor: Dr. Neda Shahidi, University of Göttingen)

  • Developed a computational framework integrating Transformer-based conditional mean modeling with low-rank latent covariance estimation to characterize time-varying population dynamics and noise correlations in macaque dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC).
  • Applied permutation-based SHAP feature attribution and null-standardized selectivity analysis to disentangle behavioral and spatial tuning across neuronal populations.

Multilayer Modeling of Alzheimer's Disease Progression

Summer 2024 - Fall 2026

Research Collaborator (Collaborator: Sobhan Khodadadi, University of Cambridge)

  • Constructed multilayer functional brain networks and evaluated graph-theoretic properties across 15 connectivity methods using data from 201 participants in the ADNI dataset.
  • Performed SHAP-based feature attribution, identifying partial-correlation-based connectivity features as prominent predictors.

Connectivity Analysis of Brain Regions for ADHD Diagnosis

Winter 2019 - Summer 2023

Research Assistant | Cognitive Neuroscience Laboratory (Supervisors: Prof. Peyvand Ghaderyan, Prof. Mousa Shamsi)

  • Developed an EEG interdependence-based connectivity feature integrated with a sparse-coding classifier for automated ADHD detection, achieving 99.17% accuracy.
  • Funded by the Cognitive Sciences and Technologies Council (CSTC), Tehran, Iran.
